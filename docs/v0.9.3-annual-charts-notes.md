# v0.9.3 Annual Charts Notes

## Summary

Version `0.9.3` adds annual chart visualizations to `Monthly Evolution` while keeping tables as the canonical source of truth.

## User-visible changes

- `Monthly Evolution` now renders annual chart panels above tables.
- Annual line charts are rendered with `Chart.js` for readable axes, legend, and tooltips.
- `Asset Total` scope shows one line chart with monthly `End Balance` only.
- `Account Groups` scope includes:
  - multi-series line evolution by group
  - pie chart (`quesitos`) for expense-oriented group composition
- `Accounts` scope now includes annual composition charts for:
  - expense-nature account share
  - income-nature account share
- `Accounts` scope includes line evolution with account selector (`All accounts` or a specific account).
- Chart-specific loading/empty/error states are explicit and non-blocking for filter changes.

## Data and semantics

- Chart datasets are derived from the same `MonthlyEvolutionReportDto` points used by table rows.
- No chart endpoint was introduced; existing monthly evolution endpoint remains the numeric source.
- Series ordering is deterministic (`DisplayName`, then `SeriesKey`) to keep stable chart identity across reloads.

## Known visualization limitations

- Multi-series evolution charts are capped to top `12` deterministic series for readability.
- Account-group composition depends on account-group nature lookup in Web layer; when lookup is unavailable, composition falls back to loaded group series.
- Liability accounts are excluded from account-group monthly evolution aggregation.
- Annual charts in `0.9.3` are year-to-date only; intra-month/daily charts are deferred to `0.9.4`.
